# Database Integrity Check — 2026-04-28

## Scope
SQLite inspection only (read-only queries) on:
- `backend/app/db/agentascend.db`
- tables: `users`, `payments`, `access_grants`, `payment_intents`

## Snapshot Counts
- users: 10
- payments: 3
- access_grants: 9
- payment_intents: 0

## Integrity Queries & Results

### 1) Duplicate `tx_signature`
- Query: grouped duplicates in `payments.tx_signature`
- Result: **none found**

### 2) Completed payments without active grant linkage
- Query: completed payments LEFT JOIN active grants on `payment_id`
- Result: **3 rows found** (all completed payments not linked by `payment_id`)
  - payment IDs: 1, 2, 3

### 3) Grants with invalid payment references
- Query: `access_grants.payment_id` set but missing matching payment
- Result: **none found**

### 4) Grants likely not backed by explicit payment rows
- Observation: all 9 grants have `payment_id` NULL; only 3 users have payment rows.
- 6 grants belong to `tg:*` users with no payment record present.
- This indicates historical/unlinked grants or alternate grant path not tied to recorded payments.

### 5) Failed/pending/unverified payment records
- Query on `payments.status in (pending,failed)` or `verification_status in (pending,failed,unverified)`
- Result: **none found**
- Additional observation: `verification_status` is NULL for all 3 payment rows.

## Schema/Timestamp/Migration Notes
- `payments` and `access_grants` tables contain expanded columns and indexes.
- `created_at` is non-null for all current rows in both tables.
- `payments.verification_status`, `confirmation_status`, `verified_at`, `amount_expected`, `amount_received` are present but currently not consistently populated by all verify paths.

## Risk Assessment

### Critical
1. **Access/payment linkage integrity gap**
   - Completed payments not linked to grants via `payment_id`.
   - Grants exist with no payment references.

### Medium
1. **Forensic metadata sparsity**
   - Verification/finality metadata columns exist but are mostly unused (NULL), limiting auditability.

### Low
1. **No active duplicates or null timestamp defects** in inspected core tables.

## Recommended Follow-ups
1. Enforce grant creation with `payment_id` + `intent_reference` for all completed payment flows.
2. Add data backfill/migration plan to link legacy grants where safely inferable.
3. Require verify routes to populate `verification_status`, `confirmation_status`, and `verified_at`.
4. Add periodic integrity job query asserting:
   - no completed payment lacks matching active grant linkage,
   - no active grant exists without valid payment (unless explicitly free/admin grant with tagged source).
