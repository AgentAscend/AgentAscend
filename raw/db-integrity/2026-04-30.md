# Database Integrity Check — 2026-04-30

## DB Source
- SQLite discovered at: `backend/app/db/agentascend.db`
- Read-only inspection only; no mutations.

## Table Presence
- `users`: present
- `payments`: present
- `access_grants`: present
- `payment_intents`: present

## Row Counts
- `users`: 10
- `payments`: 3
- `access_grants`: 9
- `payment_intents`: 0

## Integrity Queries

### Duplicate tx signatures
- Query: `GROUP BY tx_signature HAVING COUNT(*)>1`
- Result: **none found**

### Completed payments without active grant
- Query joined `payments` to active `access_grants` by `payment_id`
- Result: **3 rows** (all completed payments have no linked active grant via `payment_id`)
- Notes:
  - Existing active grants are present but use `payment_id NULL` (legacy/demo-style grants).
  - This is an integrity/auditability gap rather than direct duplicate-spend evidence.

### Active grants without valid completed payment linkage
- Query checked active grants where `payment_id IS NULL` or payment missing/non-completed
- Result: **9 rows** (all active grants currently unlinked by `payment_id`)
- Risk: weak traceability from access grant to payment evidence.

### Pending/failed payment visibility
- `payments` status distribution: `completed=3`, no `pending`/`failed` rows.
- `payment_intents` status distribution: empty table (0 rows).

## Schema/Timestamp Notes
- `payments` includes `verification_status`, `updated_at`, `verified_at` columns.
- Query result: `completed` rows with `verified_at IS NULL` = **3**.
- Suggestion: backfill `verified_at` and `verification_status` for historical completed rows where policy allows.

## Migration/Hardening Recommendations
1. Add/execute cleanup migration to attach legacy grants to canonical `payment_id` where deterministic mapping exists.
2. Enforce active-grant replay unique indexes and fail loudly if startup skips due to duplicates.
3. Add integrity cron/report query for:
   - completed payment without active grant,
   - active grant without completed payment,
   - completed payment without `verified_at`.
