## Schema observations
- `payments.tx_signature` has uniqueness enforced both by table-level unique and explicit unique index (`idx_payments_tx_signature`) — redundant but not harmful.
- `access_grants` has no `payment_id`/FK relationship to `payments`, so per-grant payment provenance cannot be enforced in DB.
- No DB-level enum/check constraints observed for `payments.status`.

## Timestamp window
- Payment records span: `2026-04-23 08:29:39` to `2026-04-23 09:11:37`
- Access grants span: `2026-04-23 08:29:39` to `2026-04-23 20:27:24`

## Migration needs
1. Ensure `payment_intents` exists on all legacy DBs before current payment routes are used.
2. Add `payment_id` linkage (and FK where feasible) to `access_grants` for auditability.
3. Normalize/constraint payment status values (check constraint or strict enum handling).
4. Consider deduplicating redundant tx-signature unique declaration if schema cleanup is planned.

## Risk summary
- **Critical:** DB/code schema drift risk due to missing `payment_intents` table in inspected SQLite snapshot.
- **Medium:** 6 active grants have no corresponding completed payment.
- **Medium:** grant provenance gap (no `payment_id` relation).
- **Low:** redundant uniqueness declaration and status constraint hardening gaps.
