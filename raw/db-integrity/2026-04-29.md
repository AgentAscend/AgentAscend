## Assessment

### Medium
- **Historical linkage gap:** payment/access rows are not fully linked by `payment_id` in existing data.
  - Impact: weaker traceability/audit joins (`payment -> grant`) for historical records.
  - Recommendation: one-time backfill migration to map grants to canonical payment rows where deterministic matching is possible.

### Low
- `payment_intents` currently empty while payment history exists.
  - Likely from legacy/test-era records prior to intent adoption.
  - Recommendation: acceptable, but document cutover date and avoid mixed semantics in analytics.

## Migration/Schema Notes
- Current schema contains replay/idempotency-supporting columns and indexes.
- Consider adding/using stricter referential constraints over time (`access_grants.payment_id` foreign-key semantics where feasible) once historical cleanup is complete.
